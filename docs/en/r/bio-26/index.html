BEHIND THE CHANCE

Weighted by life on Earth.

The draw weights Earth’s life forms by their carbon biomass. It does not use individual counts or real birth probabilities.

The 72 outcomes use group carbon estimates from the 2018 study. Within wild land mammals, 2023 species wet-mass shares provide an approximate split. Source uncertainty and differences in study coverage remain.

Group images are representative examples. The pictured species does not inherit the probability of the entire group.

The first draw is free, with 1 retry. Each new ID can earn one sharing bonus of 2 retries. Bonuses count an opened share request or a copied link, not confirmed delivery. Credits are saved in this browser.
