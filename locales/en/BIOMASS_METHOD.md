# Carbon-biomass draw methodology

This is a game using estimated carbon biomass, not individual counts or real birth probabilities. There are 72 non-overlapping result categories; plants remain one group. Viruses are excluded.

The parent-group weights use the authors’ unrounded 2018 carbon estimates: 549.0807681063422 Gt C in total, including 0.05537256615 Gt C for humans. The human chance is about 0.0100846%, or 1 in 9,916. Digits support reproducibility, not that level of ecological precision.

Wild land mammals are split into 40 species plus a residual group, preserving the 2018 parent budget and using 2023 species wet-mass shares. This hybrid model assumes equal carbon-to-wet-mass ratios within that split; it is an approximation. Marine protists and krill subdivisions preserve their parent budgets. Scientific uncertainty and survey coverage remain.

A group photo illustrates the group, not the odds of the individual pictured species. There are 50 CC0/public-domain images, 20 CC BY images and 2 AI concept illustrations. Credits, license URLs and crop notices remain on the result and share image.
